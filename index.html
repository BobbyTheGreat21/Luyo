App Workflow & Features

1. Core Philosophy

Luyo is a high-speed clinical interface designed to reduce the cognitive load on the clinician. It integrates auditory modeling, visual scaffolding (drawing), and precise quantitative tracking into a single "Live Session" flow.

2. Interaction & Visual Scaffolding

  • Canvas Layering (The New Templates):

    Tap the Layers icon to cycle through specialized clinical backgrounds:

    • Blank Canvas: Standard free-form sketching.
    • Lined Paper: For handwriting practice or orthographic cueing.
    • Tic-Tac-Toe: For quick reinforcement games during therapy "drills."
    • Oral Anatomy: A skeletal diagram for demonstrating tongue placement or articulatory gestures.
  • Auditory Modeling:

    The bottom buttons (S, R, L, TH) utilize high-fidelity text-to-speech. Use these to provide consistent auditory targets for the learner.

3. Session Intelligence (Data Tab)

The Data tab is more than just counters; it is a live analytics engine that formats your documentation in real-time.

  • Automated Percentage Logic:

    The app calculates Independence % (Unassisted vs. Total) and Accuracy % (Success with cues vs. Total) as you tap.

  • The Smart Divider:

    Your quantitative data is kept above the dashed line. Your qualitative "Observations" are kept below. The app is programmed to update the stats without overwriting your manually typed notes.

4. Best Practices

  • Use the Timer to track total session engagement time.
  • Switch to Tic-Tac-Toe every 10-15 trials as a reward.
  • Use Anatomy to show "The tongue goes here" for /r/ or /l/ sounds.
  • Copy/Paste the Session Intelligence summary directly into your EMR or SOAP notes at the end of the day.
